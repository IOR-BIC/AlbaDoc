Detailed Description

Owner of the interaction inside the views: it creates the PER (positional event router) and registers it in albaServiceLocator.

The render windows send it their mouse events, and it routes them to the tools and to the behavior of the picked VME; the tools are pushed directly on the PER. The events sent by the PER go to the listener.

See also
albaPositionalEventRouter albaRWIBase
